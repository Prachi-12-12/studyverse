SCIENCE · SAMPLE

Cells: the basics

Revise what cells are, their main parts, and why they are called the building blocks of life.

Quick revision
• The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.
• The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves the cell.
• The nucleus contains genetic material and helps control cell activities.
• Cytoplasm is where many cell reactions take place.
Study tip: Draw and label a simple cell, then explain each part in your own words.

MATHS · SAMPLE

Algebra warm-up

Remember how to simplify expressions and keep your working clear, one step at a time.

Quick revision
• Combine like terms: 3x + 2x = 5x.
• Use the distributive property: 2(x + 3) = 2x + 6.
• Keep the same operation on both sides when solving an equation.
Try: Simplify 4a + 3 − a + 2. Answer: 3a + 5.

SOCIAL SCIENCE · SAMPLE

Democracy basics

Review the core idea of democracy and why participation and fair elections matter.

Quick revision
• Democracy is a form of government in which people have a say in choosing their representatives.
• Elections should allow meaningful choice and be conducted fairly.
• Citizen participation and accountability are important democratic principles.
Think about it: Why is it important for people to have a choice?
